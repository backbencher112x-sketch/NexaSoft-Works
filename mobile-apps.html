Mobile Apps & Solutions

Step-by-step guides to fix mobile network issues, set up low-to-high WhatsApp privacy, and control YouTube video & Shorts quality.

Step 1: Quick Signal Refresh (30 Seconds)

  1. Turn on Airplane Mode for 15 seconds, then turn it off to reconnect to the nearest tower.
  2. Check that Mobile Data is turned on and Data Saver is not blocking background apps.

Step 2: Fix Preferred Network & VoLTE

  1. Open Settings → Network & Internet (or SIM Cards) → Select your SIM.
  2. Set Preferred network type to 5G/4G/3G/2G (Auto). If 5G is unstable in your area, switch to 4G/LTE for steady speed.
  3. Turn on VoLTE / 4G Calling for clear voice calls without dropping internet.

Step 3: Reset APN & Network Settings (Deep Fix)

  1. Go to SIM Settings → Access Point Names (APN) → tap the 3 dots → Reset to default.
  2. If the problem still continues, go to Settings → System → Reset options → Reset Wi-Fi, mobile & Bluetooth (this does not delete photos or apps).
  3. Restart your phone once after resetting.
Pro Tip: If only one SIM has no signal, remove the SIM tray, gently wipe the gold contacts with a dry cloth, and re-insert it.

Open WhatsApp → Settings → Privacy and choose the level that fits your need:

Level 1: Basic Protection LOW

  1. Profile Photo & About: Set to My Contacts so unknown numbers cannot see your photo.
  2. Status: Set to My Contacts (or My contacts except... to hide from specific people).
  3. Groups: Change from Everyone to My Contacts so strangers cannot add you to spam groups.

Level 2: Strong Personal Privacy MEDIUM

  1. Last Seen & Online: Set Last Seen to Nobody (or My Contacts) and Online to Same as Last Seen.
  2. Read Receipts: Turn Off to disable Blue Ticks (note: you also won't see others' blue ticks).
  3. Silence Unknown Callers: Go to Privacy → Calls and turn on Silence unknown callers.
  4. Default Message Timer: Enable Disappearing Messages (24 hours / 7 days) for temporary chats.

Level 3: Maximum Security HIGH
