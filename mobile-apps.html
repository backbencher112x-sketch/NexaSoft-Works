1. Network Problem & Slow Internet FixNo signal, 4G/5G drop, VoLTE & APN reset
▾

Step 1: Instant Tower Refresh

  1. Turn ON Airplane Mode for 20 seconds, then turn OFF to reconnect to the strongest tower.
  2. Verify Mobile Data is ON, daily quota is available, and Data Saver is OFF.

Step 2: Preferred Network & VoLTE

  1. Open Settings → Network & Internet (or SIM Cards) → Select SIM.
  2. Set Preferred network to 5G/4G/3G/2G (Auto). If 5G fluctuates indoors, switch to 4G/LTE for steady speed.
  3. Turn ON VoLTE / 4G Calling and Wi-Fi Calling.

Step 3: Deep APN & Network Reset

  1. Go to SIM → Access Point Names (APN) → 3 dots → Reset to default.
  2. Still slow? Go to Settings → System → Reset options → Reset Wi-Fi, mobile & Bluetooth and restart your phone.
Pro Tip: Resetting network settings never deletes your photos, contacts, or apps.

2. Storage Clean & Free SpaceClear cache, WhatsApp junk & hidden trash
▾

Step 1: Safe App Cache Cleaning

  1. Open Settings → Apps → See all apps and sort by Size.
  2. Open Instagram, YouTube, Chrome, Telegram → Storage & cache → Clear cache (do NOT tap Clear Storage).

Step 2: WhatsApp & Telegram Cleanup

  1. In WhatsApp → Settings → Storage and data → Manage storage, delete unwanted videos Larger than 5 MB and Forwarded many times.
  2. In Telegram → Data & Storage → Storage Usage, tap Clear Cache.

Step 3: Empty Trash & Old Downloads

  1. Empty Trash / Bin in Gallery, Google Photos, and Files by Google (deleted items still take space for 30–60 days).
Pro Tip: Keep at least 15–20% storage free so your phone never lags.

3. WhatsApp & Phone Privacy (Low to High)Basic, Medium & Maximum lockdown guide
▾

Level 1 (Low / Basic): Everyday Privacy

  1. Open WhatsApp → Settings → Privacy.
  2. Set Profile Photo, About, Status & Groups to My Contacts so strangers cannot view or add you to spam groups.

Level 2 (Medium): Personal Control

  1. Set Last Seen to Nobody and Online to Same as Last Seen.
  2. Turn OFF Read Receipts (Blue Ticks) and turn ON Silence unknown callers under Privacy → Calls.

Level 3 (High / Maximum): Full Security

  1. Enable Settings → Account → Two-step verification (6-digit PIN + recovery email).
  2. Turn ON Fingerprint App Lock and lock sensitive chats with Chat Lock + Secret Code.
  3. Under Privacy → Advanced, turn ON Protect IP address in calls & Disable link previews.
  4. Check Android Settings → Privacy → Permission manager to remove unnecessary Camera/Mic permissions.

6. Keyboard Theme & Custom PhotoGboard photo background & Hindi typing
▾

Step 1: Set Photo or Dark Theme (Gboard)

  1. Open keyboard → tap Gear (Settings) → Theme.
  2. Tap + (My Themes) to pick any Gallery photo, set brightness to 45–60% for readable keys, and tap Apply.
  3. Toggle Key borders ON/OFF for your favorite style.

Step 2: Fast Hindi / Hinglish Typing

  1. Go to Keyboard Settings → Languages → Add keyboard → Hindi (India) → abc → हिन्दी.
  2. Long-press the Spacebar anytime to switch between English & Hindi.
